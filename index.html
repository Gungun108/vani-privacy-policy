Vani – Privacy Policy

How Vani handles microphone access and your tracking information.

Effective date: 5 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Vani ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use Vani.

1. What Vani does

Vani helps users track occurrences of abusive language and spoken instances of the name “Ram” during a tracking session. Results and daily history are intended to help users review their own speech habits.

2. Microphone access and background tracking

When you tap Start Tracking and grant microphone permission, Vani uses the device microphone to analyze speech for its tracking features. Tracking may continue when the app is minimized or the screen is off, until you stop tracking.

While tracking is active, Android may display an ongoing notification or other system indicator. You can stop tracking from the app or from the controls provided in that notification, where available. You can also revoke microphone permission in Android settings; doing so may stop the feature from working.

Important: Only start tracking when you are comfortable with microphone monitoring. Be considerate of people nearby and comply with applicable laws. Avoid recording or monitoring others without their knowledge or consent.

3. On-device processing and storage

Based on the app's current design, microphone audio is processed on your device. Daily counts and tracking history are stored locally on your device. Vani's core tracking feature does not send microphone audio or this tracking history to Vani-operated servers.

Local information may be removed if you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. The exact deletion behavior depends on your device and app version.

4. Information sharing and third-party services

Vani does not sell your microphone audio or local tracking history. This policy describes the core tracking feature. If a future version adds analytics, crash reporting, advertising, cloud backup, account features, or other third-party SDKs that collect or transmit information, this policy and the app's Google Play Data safety disclosures will be updated before those features are released.

5. Permissions and your choices

  • Microphone permission is used for voice tracking after you choose to start it.
  • You can stop a tracking session at any time.
  • You can manage or revoke microphone permission in your Android device settings.
  • You can clear locally stored app data through Android settings; this may delete your tracking history.

6. Children and age suitability

Vani is intended for users aged 13 and older, subject to the age requirements and laws applicable in the user's country. Vani is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Users should not use Vani to monitor another person. Parents or guardians should guide minors in using microphone-enabled features responsibly.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps in designing the app to limit the handling of voice data. Because tracking history is stored on the device, users should protect access to their device and use its available security features.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app's features or data practices change. We will publish the updated version at this same URL and revise the effective date.
